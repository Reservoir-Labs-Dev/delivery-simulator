# KIU Bachelor Thesis Guidelines — Compliance Notes

Source: _Rules for the Preparing and Defending a Bachelor's Thesis_, Kutaisi International University, Article 4.

## 1. Required document structure (Article 4.1)

The thesis must contain these components, in order:

### a) Title Page (per Annex 4)

- Institution + school + program name: **Sylfaen, 14pt, bold and regular**
- Student full name (centered, 2cm spacing): **Sylfaen, 16pt, bold and regular**
- Thesis title (2cm below name, centered): **16pt, bold and regular**
- Academic degree (3cm below title): **Sylfaen, 14pt, regular**
- Supervisor full name (3–4cm below, centered) + academic position (1–2cm below name): **12pt, regular**
- Place and year (bottom center, e.g. "Kutaisi, 2026"): **12pt, regular**

### b) Declaration page

Signed originality statement with this exact wording:

> "I, the author of this bachelor's thesis, declare that the thesis is my own original work and does not contain any material previously published, submitted for publication, or presented for defense by other authors, unless properly referenced or cited in accordance with academic standards."

Followed by student name(s) and date.

### c) Abstract

- Starts on a new page
- 150–200 words
- Must include: introduction, main content, conclusion
- Must cover all essential aspects of the thesis
- Must NOT include content not found in the main body
- Citations NOT allowed
- In Georgian and English (Georgian first), UNLESS thesis is entirely in English — then English only
- Followed by keywords

### d) Table of Contents

- All chapters, sections, subsections in sequential order
- Front matter (excluding title page): Roman numerals (i, ii, iii…)
- Main body onwards: Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3…)
- Include lists of figures / tables / illustrations if applicable

### e) Main Text (four sections)

1. **Introduction** — relevance of topic, research objectives, subject of study, practical significance, clearly articulated research problem
2. **Literature Review** — arguments and insights from other scholars, linked to research question; sources include academic articles, dissertations, books, monographs
3. **Methodology and Results** — research process, results obtained, analysis
4. **Conclusion** — summary of key findings and their significance

### f) Final Pages

- **Bibliography** — all sources in alphabetical order, Georgian sources first
- **Appendices** — supplementary materials (tables, charts, questionnaires, other documents)

## 2. Language (Article 4.2)

Thesis may be written in Georgian or English.

## 3. Formatting requirements (Article 4.3)

- **Length:** 30–50 pages
- **Font:** Times New Roman, Arial, Calibri, or Sylfaen
- **Font size:** 12pt
- **Line spacing:** 1.5
- **Margins:** left 3cm, right 1.5cm, top 2.5cm, bottom 2.5cm

## 4. Citation style (Article 4.4)

APA or IEEE. **We use IEEE.**

## 5. Pre-defense and submission rules (Article 5)

- Final thesis submitted electronically before defense
- Filename: `Bachelor's Thesis – Student's Full Name`
- Plagiarism review happens after submission, before defense
- **Article 5.6: misconduct found in the FINAL version means loss of right to revise/resubmit** — run our own plagiarism check first
- Thesis sent to defense committee no later than one week before defense
- Presentation materials submitted alongside thesis
- Defense must occur in the same semester thesis work started (Article 7.2)
